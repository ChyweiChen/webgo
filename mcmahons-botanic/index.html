Our story

Come for the coffee.
Stay for the feeling.

McMahon's is the kind of place you stumble upon once and keep coming back to. Tucked along Botanic Avenue, we're a small, independent café with a big heart — the sort of spot where the staff remember your order and the cakes are baked fresh each morning.

Whether you've just wandered the Botanic Gardens across the road, or you're meeting a friend for a long catch-up, there's a table with your name on it — out front on the street, or in our leafy courtyard out back.
