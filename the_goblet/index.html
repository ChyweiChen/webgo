Since the first pint

A proper local.

Old-fashioned in all the right ways.

The Goblet is the kind of pub you can settle into. Three spaces to choose from — the front bar for a quick one, a long, cosy lounge for a proper sit-down, and an upstairs room when the night calls for a bit more room.

There's a big fire, traditional wood and brass throughout, and a welcome that doesn't depend on whether you're a regular. Yet. Step-free access throughout and a wheelchair-accessible car park mean everyone can join in.

"A really nice spot, worth the trip."Based on Google reviews

Why people come back

Known for the good stuff.

No gimmicks. Just the things a proper Irish pub should do well — done well.

A proper Guinness

The pint people talk about. Pulled carefully, poured with a good head, and worth coming back for.

Sunday carvery

Roast beef, turkey and all the trimmings. Generous plates and the kind of lunch that sends you home happy.

Live music

Bands and a bit of craic, especially at weekends. Ask in the pub for who's playing this week.

Room for everyone

Free parking, outdoor seating, screens for the match, and full wheelchair access to every part of the pub.

Pints, food & the match
