Where the music does the talking.

Pipers Corner is a proper old-fashioned pub on Marlborough Street — the kind of place where the night is made by the people in it and the tunes coming from the corner.

It's cosy, often standing-room-only, and all the better for it. Settle in for a traditional session, a craft beer or a perfectly poured pint, and let the evening unfold.

"The music is outstanding! Worth every bit to hear traditional Irish music."Based on Google reviews
✦   The heart of the house

Live music,most nights.

Trad sessions are what people come for — fiddles, whistles, pipes and a crowd that's always up for a singalong. Reviews regularly mention live music from around 9pm, and there's no cover to get in.
